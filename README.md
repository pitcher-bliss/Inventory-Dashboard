Inventory Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Inventory Analytics Dashboard designed to monitor, analyze, and visualize inventory performance across multiple warehouses.

The dashboard provides key insights into total inventory value, stock quantities, sales performance, purchase values, average unit prices, and low-stock items. It features interactive filters for product categories, warehouses, and stock status, allowing users to explore inventory data and make informed business decisions.

Key features include:

Inventory value and quantity tracking.
Monthly sales versus purchase trend analysis.
Inventory value breakdown by product category.
Top 10 products above reorder level.
Inventory turnover analysis by category.
Stock status breakdown (In Stock, Low Stock, and Out of Stock).
Purchase value analysis by supplier.
Stock quantity comparison across warehouses.

Objective: To simplify inventory monitoring, identify stock shortages, track purchasing and sales trends, and support data-driven inventory management decisions.
